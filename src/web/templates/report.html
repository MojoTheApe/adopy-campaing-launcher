How to run the RPA

Connect Multilogin and link the account to its profile here. Then open Campaign Builder, preview the campaign and confirm the draft run.

The RPA needs a profile with CDP access. It leaves the Multilogin window open so you can check the draft and the selected Facebook account. Keep it open during the run.
