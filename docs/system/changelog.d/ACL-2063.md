## ACL-2063 — Account refresh queue: structure-only read and one-at-a-time queue — 2026-10-23

First slice of the scheduled account reads (design and plan under `docs/superpowers/`). Nothing is wired to the dashboard yet; no profile is opened by this change.
- `campaign_inventory`: a read of the campaign structure only (campaigns, ad sets, ads, statuses, budgets, bid strategy) with no Insights, period or country split; a failed attempt now stores its Meta stop kind and UTC time.
- `account_refresh`: a server-side queue that reads one account at a time (priority action, retry, attention, schedule), skips accounts read or failed less than an hour ago (also re-checked when a request is taken), and halts on global stops with a single reason. A checkpoint or call-budget halt survives a restart and is seen by a second dashboard process; a checkpoint halt is lifted only by a successful read of the account that raised it.
Remaining slices of the task: window scheduler and `ACL_AUTO_REFRESH`, refresh on attention, refresh after commands, UI (row status, Retry, banner, removal of Read spend / Read campaigns), telemetry triggers.
