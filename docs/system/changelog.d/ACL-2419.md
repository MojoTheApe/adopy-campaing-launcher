## ACL-2419 — Home accounts: status comes from Meta, a stale "no warning" is never shown as fact — 2026-11-09

The campaign-list read already fetches the ad account (`account_status`, `disable_reason`); it now also stores that status (no extra request), so the status refreshes with every list read and with Open. A `clear` status older than 24 hours, or followed by a failed read, is shown as **unknown** with its date instead of "no warning"/"active"; disabled, restricted, review and blocked statuses stay visible and are marked "not refreshed". Home counts an account with such a stale or failed `clear` as **Unknown** even if it has spend, and the State cell shows the status word, the read time and the disable reason. After **Open** succeeds, one read goes through the existing refresh queue (same lease, pause, cooldown and call budget; no timer or polling).
