## ACL-2406 — Builder: ads 2+ take only Primary text; Headline and Description come from Ad 1 — 2026-11-11

Ad 1 (the first ad of the campaign) now defines the Headline and Description through its Primary text 1. Ads 2 and later show no Headline line in the builder (only Primary texts) and are published with Ad 1's Headline and Description, also for their texts 2-5. Removing Ad 1 makes the next ad the source. The pre-flight audit requires and measures the Headline only for Ad 1; the card preview of ads 2+ shows Ad 1's Headline.
