## ACL-2188 — Instant Form templates: background image — 2026-11-04

A form template can carry an optional background image: a picture that already exists on the Page, chosen by its Photo ID (`context_card.cover_photo_id`). No file is uploaded and no new write edge is added. The picture is chosen with "Choose from Page" (the Page's form covers and uploaded photos, one read per press) or by pasting a Photo ID, in the Forms tab and in the builder's "Create and save form". It needs a Description, belongs to one Page, and a template used on another Page stops at Check plan before anything is written. A changed picture makes a new form on the next publish; a template without a picture is unchanged (same hash, same request). The picker shows text rows only, no thumbnails. A cover that cannot be confirmed on a new form stops the run; on a reused form it is only noted. Clearing the picture now really removes it from the stored template.
Risk: a build older than this one shares the template library. It drops the picture when it validates a template, so it publishes a form without a picture under the old name and does not warn.
