## ACL-2221 — Use Facebook Page identity on Instagram — 2026-10-26

Check plan can plan use of the selected Facebook Page on Instagram without requiring a separate linked Instagram account. It stays read-only and warns that the Page-backed identity will be prepared on Publish. Publication verifies that identity before any campaign/media/form writes, preserves Instagram placements, and never guesses an Instagram id. Closed Python/JS policies pin the exact Page; a durable intent prevents repeating an unconfirmed preparation across runs. Existing linked identities remain preferred. Meta permissions and live preparation/readback still require account-specific verification.
